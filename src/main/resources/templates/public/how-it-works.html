How it works

Book a JCB in four steps

1 · Create an account

Register as a customer and verify your details. Owners and operators can also register to join the platform.

2 · Find a machine

Search and filter available JCBs by type, location and date, then check real-time availability.

3 · Request a booking

Pick your dates and submit a booking request. The admin reviews and approves it — conflicts are prevented automatically.

4 · Get the job done

A verified operator is assigned, job status is tracked, and an invoice is generated on completion.
